Passage 1: Football stars at school (Manchester United)

Can you imagine being a famous football star but still attending school every Monday morning as usual? That's what some students at Ashton-on-Mersey School in England do! The school is about 7 km from Old Trafford... All football clubs have to make sure players under 18 are getting an education... Young players have classes two days a week and study a range of different subjects. They still follow the rules like all the other students in the school. The football club has given money to the school... When one French-speaking player came back to visit, he joined a French class, helping students improve language skills!

Passage 3: Bees, bees, bees (The natural world)

Most people enjoy seeing bees in gardens and parks, but it can be scary when they're flying together in large numbers, and it can be even more frightening if they come a bit too close!

A few years ago, Lisa Turnbull was in her home in York. She had made a cake and left it on the kitchen table. She was looking forward to eating it, but when she opened the kitchen window to help the cake cool more quickly, she unfortunately didn't notice the large number of bees flying around in her back garden. A few minutes later, Lisa heard a loud noise coming from her kitchen. She opened the door and saw a huge swarm of bees all over her kitchen table. The bees had left their old home and were looking for a new one. It seems that the queen bee, which controls all the others, had fancied a bit of cake and landed on it. She was followed by 15,000 others! Luckily, Lisa knew a bit about bees, so she knew they weren't dangerous and she didn't need to be afraid. She calmly closed the door and called a local beekeeper who found the situation very amusing and took the bees away. Although her cake wasn't badly damaged, Lisa didn't fancy eating it!

Last summer, John Norton from Manchester had a similar experience with a large number of bees. He was driving home after a long day at work when he noticed thousands of insects following his car. He realised after a while that they were bees and stopped his car, hoping that they would continue flying on and leave him in peace. However, when he stopped the car they landed on the back of it and stayed there. Feeling rather nervous, John used his phone to look online and find the phone number of a local beekeeper. John called him, and the beekeeper arrived half an hour later. It turned out that the queen bee had flown into the boot of the car while John was putting some shopping into it. When he closed the boot, she was stuck inside, so when he drove off, the rest of the bees followed. They somehow knew where their queen was even though they couldn't see her. John stayed in his car and didn't get out until the beekeeper had safely removed all the bees – which took over three hours! That's a good excuse for being late home!

✉️ Writing Part 2 (Short Message 35 - 45 words)

Đề 1 (Trang 49): Xin lỗi bạn George vì không đi xem phim được

Yêu cầu: Xin lỗi, giải thích lý do, đề xuất một dịp hẹn khác.

Hi George,
I'm really sorry but I can't go to the cinema with you this Friday because I have to study for my exams. Why don't we go on Saturday afternoon instead? Let me know if that's OK for you.
Best,
Linh
Đề 2 (Trang 51): Rủ bạn Jenna đến quán cà phê mới mở

Yêu cầu: Kể về quán đã đến, món/điều thích nhất, rủ bạn đi cùng.

Hi Jenna,
Last weekend I visited a wonderful new café in the town centre. I loved their hot chocolate and delicious strawberry cakes! Would you like to go there with me this Sunday afternoon? See you soon!
Linh
Đề 3 (Trang 52): Kể cho bạn Tom về kỳ nghỉ vừa rồi

Hi Tom,
I've just got back from a fantastic holiday in Da Nang! I really enjoyed relaxing on the beach and trying the local seafood. Would you like to come round soon so I can show you my holiday photos?

📝 Writing Part 3 (Letter / Story ~100 words)

Đề viết thư (Trang 55): Khuyên bạn cải thiện mối quan hệ gia đình
Dear Sam,
I'm sorry to hear that you had an argument with your father and that your little sister annoyed you. Disagreements happen in every family, so you don't need to worry too much.
I usually get on very well with my parents because we talk openly whenever there is a problem. In my opinion, you should apologize to your dad for getting angry and explain your feelings calmly. You could also organize a movie night or cook dinner together to cheer everyone up!
Write soon and let me know how it goes.
Best wishes,
Linh
Đề viết truyện (Trang 57): Bắt đầu bằng câu cho sẵn
I saw the advert for a talent show and decided to apply. I had always dreamed of singing on a big stage, so I chose my favourite acoustic song to perform.
On the audition day, I felt extremely nervous while waiting for my turn backstage. When they called my name, the bright lights almost blinded me! However, as soon as the guitar started playing, my confidence returned. The judges smiled and clapped when I finished.
Finally, they told me I had passed to the live round. It was the most unforgettable moment of my life!
Đề viết truyện (Trang 59): Nhan đề "A new home"
A new home
Last year, my family decided to move to a new city because of my dad's job. At first, I was upset because I had to leave all my friends behind. I didn't want to talk to anyone at my new school.
However, on my second day, a friendly classmate called Mai invited me to sit with her group at lunch. We quickly discovered that we both loved drawing and K-pop music. Over the next few weeks, Mai introduced me to the rest of the class, and I slowly started to feel comfortable.
By the end of the term, I had made lots of new friends and joined the school's art club. Now I can't imagine living anywhere else!
